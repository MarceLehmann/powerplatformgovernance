## About this site

### What is Power Platform Governance?

A curated collection of Power Platform governance articles from different blogs, brought into one place. Each entry is a short summary with a clear governance takeaway and a direct link to the original article. See the [About page]({{ '/about/' | relative_url }}) for the full editorial approach.

### Do you republish full articles?

No. We write an independent short summary and a governance takeaway, and link directly to the original. We never reproduce or translate a full article.

### How do you choose what to feature?

Every candidate goes through an automated relevance check before it is summarized: it has to be substantially about Power Platform governance, security, compliance, DLP, environments, ALM, ownership, CoE, Dataverse, Power Apps, Power Automate or Copilot Studio administration, with enough original content to summarize responsibly. Generic AI news or marketing does not pass.

No. This is an independent project edited by [Marcel Lehmann](https://www.linkedin.com/in/marcelehmann/), a Microsoft MVP and founder of [KMUpower](https://kmupower.com/). It is not an official Microsoft publication.

## Power Platform governance basics

### What is Power Platform governance, in practice?

The set of decisions and controls around who can build what, where, with which data and connectors: environment strategy, Data Loss Prevention (DLP) policies, ALM and ownership of what gets built. It's less about restricting makers and more about making sure the platform stays manageable as adoption grows.

### Do I need Premium licensing for basic governance?

No. Environment strategy and DLP policies are set by admins in the Power Platform admin center and need no Premium licence for your makers. Security roles apply in environments with Dataverse. Premium connectors, Dataverse and Managed Environments (which need licences with Premium use rights) become relevant for specific controls, not for governance as a whole.

### What's the difference between governance and a Center of Excellence (CoE)?

Governance is the set of rules and controls themselves. A CoE is the team and the tooling (often Microsoft's CoE Starter Kit) that applies those rules in practice: inventories, approvals, nudges and reporting. You can have governance without a formal CoE team, especially in smaller organizations.

### What's the difference between Power Platform Governance and Power Platform Daily?

Different scope, same curation approach. Governance covers the administrative side: security, compliance, DLP, ALM, environments and Center of Excellence. Our sister site [Power Platform Daily](https://powerplatformdaily.com/) covers product news and how-to guidance for Copilot Studio, Power Apps, Power Automate, Power BI and Fabric. An article only ever appears on one of the two sites.
