Power Platform Governance is a publication for the people responsible for making Microsoft Power Platform useful, secure and maintainable.

## What we cover

- Environment strategy, platform ownership and adoption
- Data policies, connectors and security
- Solutions, ALM and production operations
- Dataverse, Power Apps, Power Automate and Copilot Studio governance

## Editorial approach

We favour practical guidance with clear scope and useful takeaways. Community submissions are reviewed before publication. When we feature external content, we credit the source and link to the original article; the original remains on its author's website.

The initial articles on this site are introductory examples from Power Platform Governance. Product capabilities change, so verify requirements against current Microsoft documentation before applying changes in production.

## Editor

The site is edited by **Marcel Lehmann**, founder of [KMUpower](https://kmupower.com/), a Swiss Power Platform consultancy and training business.
