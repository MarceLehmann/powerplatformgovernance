## TL;DR

Copilot Studio plugins connect Copilot to capabilities and can be built with little code. The episode explains what plugins are, how Copilot decides when to use them, how connectors and agents relate to Copilot Studio, and which mistakes, limits, costs and control questions matter.

{% include curated-attribution.html %}

## Governance takeaway

- Makers: Learn the plugin vocabulary, Copilot selection process, and relationship between connectors, agents, and Copilot Studio before building; this reduces confusion during low-code creation.
- Admins/CoE: Review the stated mistakes, limits, costs, and control responsibilities before adoption; these considerations shape how plugins are introduced and managed.
- Leadership/Business: Clarify who controls plugins and what they cost before approving use; both topics are identified as part of practical adoption.
