## TL;DR

New Microsoft 365 Copilot Business purchases may automatically create a default spending policy for pay-as-you-go AI services when no default exists. The source warns that group-based scope may include All Users, while limits are unclear; it cites 1,000 credits per member user and a possible 75,000-credit, $750 monthly policy for 75 users.

{% include curated-attribution.html %}

## Governance takeaway

- Admins/CoE: Check every new Microsoft 365 Copilot Business purchase for an automatically created default spending policy, its All Users scope, and its credit limits, because existing defaults prevent this setup and the stated limits remain unclear.
- Leadership/Business: Treat usage-based billing as a budget risk; the source gives a possible example where five licenses in a 75-user tenant could create a 75,000-credit, $750 monthly policy, compared with a $100 monthly license purchase.
