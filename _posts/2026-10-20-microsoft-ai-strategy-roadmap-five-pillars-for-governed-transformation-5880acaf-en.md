## TL;DR

Microsoft’s AI Strategy Roadmap defines five value drivers: business strategy, technology and data, AI experience, organization and culture, and governance and security. It recommends measurable use cases, reliable data, controlled experimentation, reusable components, executive alignment, agent permissions, responsible AI, lifecycle inventory, observability and audit.

## Governance takeaway

- Makers: Start with a limited, measurable use case and use controlled environments, so experimentation exposes hallucinations, prompt vulnerabilities and integration errors before broad deployment.
- Admins/CoE: Build shared platforms, reusable patterns, inventories and lifecycle controls for agents, so teams can scale without losing identity, permission, cost or operational oversight.
- Security/Compliance: Define agent identities, permissions, responsible AI controls, observability and audit requirements early, because agents can turn data errors into operational errors.
- Leadership/Business: Link AI investments to measurable business outcomes and review the portfolio regularly, rather than treating every successful pilot as a reason to scale.
