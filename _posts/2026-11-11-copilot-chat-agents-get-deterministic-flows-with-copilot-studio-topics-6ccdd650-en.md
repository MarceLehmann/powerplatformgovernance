## TL;DR

Copilot Studio topics give Copilot Chat agents deterministic conversational flows for scenarios such as HR policy answers that must follow approved wording. MC1518233 also names connected agents, evaluations, monitoring, a native Copilot Chat preview, and lifecycle management across development, test, and production, with general availability stated as October 26.

## Governance takeaway

- Makers: Use Copilot Studio topics when an agent must follow approved wording, such as an HR policy answer, rather than relying only on model-led conversation.
- Admins/CoE: Plan for evaluations, monitoring, connected agents, and lifecycle management across development, test, and production as the announced capabilities become available.
- Security/Compliance: Identify responses that require tight conversational control and assess whether deterministic topics are needed to keep the answer on script.
