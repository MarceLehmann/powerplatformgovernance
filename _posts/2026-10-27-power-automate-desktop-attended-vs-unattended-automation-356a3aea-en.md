## TL;DR

Power Automate Desktop offers attended automation that runs while you watch and unattended automation that runs while you are away. The episode explains how unattended robots sign in, how to choose between the two modes, why their costs differ and where beginners commonly get the choice wrong.

## Governance takeaway

- Makers: Decide whether the automation needs you at the desk or must run while you are away, because that determines the suitable robot type.
- Admins/CoE: Compare attended and unattended operation before standardising a desktop automation, including how the unattended robot signs in and runs alone.
- Leadership/Business: Check the cost difference between the two robot types before selecting an approach, since the episode warns that they do not cost the same.
