## TL;DR

Use the SharePoint admin center and activity reports to sort sites by last activity and storage, define an inactivity rule such as 90 days for team sites, ask owners, check retention and legal holds, move sites to read-only, then archive or delete with approval and a recorded decision.

## Governance takeaway

- Makers: Confirm whether site content is still needed when asked, because owners decide whether a site should be kept, archived or deleted.
- Admins/CoE: Export site owners, last activity and storage, apply a written rule per site type, and repeat the review cycle to prevent backlog.
- Security/Compliance: Check retention policies, legal holds, linked Teams and open access dependencies before archival or deletion.
