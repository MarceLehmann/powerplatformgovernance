## TL;DR

SharePoint Online sites, hubs, content types, metadata, navigation and permissions determine what Copilot can find and see. Structure this information architecture so content remains findable, governable and safe to surface. The episode targets SharePoint architects, information managers, Microsoft 365 admins and Copilot rollout teams.

## Governance takeaway

- Admins/CoE: Review sites, hubs, content types, metadata, navigation and permissions so Copilot can surface content safely.
- Security/Compliance: Treat permissions as a Copilot visibility control, because Copilot can only see what permissions allow.
