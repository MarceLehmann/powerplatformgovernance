## TL;DR

Power Platform can support a nonconformance process from defect capture and triage through approvals, corrective and preventive actions, and reporting. The process should be modelled with quality teams and connected to existing systems, helping address the cost of workflows that are slow or unclear.

{% include curated-attribution.html %}

## Governance takeaway

- Makers: Model the nonconformance process around capture, triage, approvals, corrective and preventive actions, and reporting so the workflow is clear from defect detection to action.
- Leadership/Business: Treat a slow or unclear path from detection to corrective action as a cost risk, and involve quality teams and existing systems when shaping the process.
