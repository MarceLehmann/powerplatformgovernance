## TL;DR

Unmanaged developer environments, including free Power Apps Developer Plan environments, are suspended on 15 December 2026 and deleted on 15 January 2027. Admins can filter Type = Developer and Managed = No in the Power Platform admin center. Makers should export their solutions and move them to a managed environment, sandbox or trial.

{% include curated-attribution.html %}

## Governance takeaway

- Makers: Put apps, flows and other components into your own unmanaged solution, then export it before 15 December so the work remains available.
- Admins/CoE: Filter environments by Type = Developer and Managed = No, then identify active environments and plan managed conversion or migration.
- Leadership/Business: Provide a licensed managed environment or organizational sandbox before suspension to avoid unavailable apps, flows, sites and data.
