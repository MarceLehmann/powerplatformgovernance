## TL;DR

Dynamics 365 remains the system of record while Copilot, Teams and AI agents provide connected access and execute tasks. The source highlights 30 predefined Dynamics 365 skills, CRM integration in Teams, Service Agent and Sales Development Agent, and the need to govern agent permissions, approvals, limits and traceability before production use.

## Governance takeaway

- Makers: Define which agent actions need human approval and which can run autonomously, because execution changes require explicit process boundaries.
- Admins/CoE: Review permissions, operational limits, integrations and traceability before deployment, because agents can create cases, send emails and update CRM data.
- Security/Compliance: Check access to shared Microsoft 365 and Dynamics 365 context, because Work IQ connects customer data with meetings, emails, documents and Teams channels.
- Leadership/Business: Pilot measurable processes and track cost, quality and outcomes, because agent counts alone do not demonstrate business success.
