## TL;DR

SharePoint Advanced Management gives admins site ownership policies, inactive-site attestations, permission and sharing-link reports, and sensitivity-label snapshots. The author's five-step starting point: fix ownership, review lifecycle, map access, detect oversharing, then validate all three with evidence before any Copilot rollout touches that content.

{% include curated-attribution.html %}

## Governance takeaway

Admins/CoE should run SAM's ownership, lifecycle, and access reports before any Copilot rollout, since fixing ownership and mapping access is the actual prerequisite work, not a side task. Security/Compliance should treat the oversharing and sensitivity-label reports as the evidence a Copilot go-live needs, not paperwork to backfill afterward. Leadership/Business should hold the rollout date until that evidence exists, since an agent will act on whatever ownership and access assumptions are already in place, wrong or not.
