## TL;DR

Copilot Studio can add a human-in-the-loop step to an agent: the agent sends a message through Teams or email, collects the person’s response, and uses that response to decide its next action when situations are unexpected or too risky for autonomous decisions.

{% include curated-attribution.html %}

## Governance takeaway

- Makers: Add a human-in-the-loop step where an agent may face unexpected situations or decisions that are too risky to make alone.
- Security/Compliance: Watch for autonomous decisions that require human oversight, and use the Teams or email response step to keep a person involved.
- Leadership/Business: Identify decisions that agents must not make independently, because the source warns that planning cannot cover every situation.
