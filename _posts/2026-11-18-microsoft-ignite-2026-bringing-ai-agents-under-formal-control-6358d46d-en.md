## TL;DR

Microsoft Ignite 2026 puts AI agents under formal control: Agent 365 adds dynamic risk evaluation, orphan-agent detection, business classification and control of plugins, skills and MCP servers, while Entra strengthens agent identity and Conditional Access, Intune discovers local agents, Purview extends data controls for Copilot and agents, and Microsoft Perception reaches general availability. Adoption should separate general availability, Frontier, preview and announced features.

## Governance takeaway

- Admins/CoE: Use Agent 365 to detect orphaned agents, classify them by business area and control plugins, skills and MCP servers; basic metrics come without license, full governance stays behind it.
- Security/Compliance: Ground every agent in verifiable Entra identity with Conditional Access, least privilege, audit and a defined incident response, and use Purview controls to protect sensitive data in Copilot scenarios.
- Leadership/Business: Choose two or three processes with measurable problems and define what data the agent needs, which actions it may run, how success is measured and who answers when it fails.
- Makers: Separate general availability, Frontier, preview and announced states in adoption plans so roadmaps do not promise features that cannot be deployed yet.
