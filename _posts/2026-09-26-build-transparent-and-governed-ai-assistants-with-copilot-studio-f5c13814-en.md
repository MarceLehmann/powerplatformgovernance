## TL;DR

Copilot Studio citizen developers should create transparent, explainable assistants, document AI workflows and decisions, promote accessibility, and follow governance rules supporting inclusion, fairness, and ethical decision-making. The source also encourages sharing components and best practices to make low-code AI more open and collaborative.

{% include curated-attribution.html %}

## Governance takeaway

- Makers: Build Copilot Studio assistants that are explainable, accessible, and transparent so people can understand how they work.
- Admins/CoE: Encourage documentation, AI literacy, shared components, and governance rules for inclusion, fairness, and ethical decisions.
- Security/Compliance: Push for visibility into AI decisions and transparency about how models are trained and what data is used.
- Leadership/Business: Challenge AI initiatives to demonstrate meaningful human value rather than simply optimizing profit or adding automation.
