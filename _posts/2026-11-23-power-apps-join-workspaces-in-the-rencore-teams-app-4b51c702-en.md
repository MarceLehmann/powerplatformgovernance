## TL;DR

Power Apps now appear in Workspaces in the Rencore Teams App, next to Teams, SharePoint sites, Viva Engage communities and Microsoft 365 groups. From one list inside Microsoft Teams, owners can see who owns and uses each app, manage access, and complete scheduled access reviews whose decisions are recorded as evidence.

## Governance takeaway

- Makers: Your app now sits next to your teams and sites in Workspaces; check who owns and uses it and tighten over-sharing on the spot instead of raising a ticket with IT.
- Admins/CoE: Set up Power Apps reviews in the Review Builder the same way as Teams and SharePoint; one list means one habit to build and one rollout instead of a new one per workload.
- Security/Compliance: Scheduled access reviews make owners confirm who still needs access, and every decision is recorded as evidence for audits.
