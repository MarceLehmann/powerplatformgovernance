## TL;DR

Power Platform architects should ask why before choosing a solution, Keith Atherton of Capgemini tells CRM Rocks in episode 167. Becoming an architect means more than understanding technology: discovery, documentation, business value and architectural decisions matter, and AI accelerating development makes testing, validation and strong foundations even more important.

{% include curated-attribution.html %}

## Governance takeaway

- Makers: Ask why the solution is needed before picking technology, and document what you find; architecture involves far more than understanding the tech.
- Admins/CoE: On rescue projects and mid-project entries, give architects room to build trust with customers and development teams before restructuring.
- Leadership/Business: With AI accelerating development and documentation, plan for stronger testing, validation and architectural foundations.
