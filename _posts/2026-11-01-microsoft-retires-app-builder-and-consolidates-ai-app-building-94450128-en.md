## TL;DR

Microsoft retires the App Builder agent and vibe.powerapps.com on November 30. Apps worth keeping can move through an in-product migration tool. Chat-based building shifts to Copilot Cowork and Copilot Studio, with personal developer environments, DLP and an admin inventory, but the replacement remains preview-based and Managed Runtime licensing is enforced after 20 operations.

## Governance takeaway

- Makers: Identify processes relying on App Builder or vibe apps and plan migration for apps the business needs to retain.
- Admins/CoE: Use the new path&\#39;s personal developer environments, DLP and admin inventory to manage the transition.
- Leadership/Business: Treat the replacement as preview technology and account for Copilot Credits or Power Apps Premium when people run apps on Managed Runtime.
