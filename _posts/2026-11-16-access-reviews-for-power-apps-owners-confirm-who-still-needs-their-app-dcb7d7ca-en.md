## TL;DR

Rencore has extended Reviews to Power Apps. On a schedule set once by admins, app owners confirm who can use each app in the Rencore Teams App in Microsoft Teams, alongside Teams and SharePoint reviews. Reviews include people who reach the app through security groups. Owners keep users, hand the app to a new owner or retire it, with every decision recorded for IT.

## Governance takeaway

- Admins/CoE: Add Power Apps to the Review Builder next to Teams and SharePoint, set the schedule once and use the recorded owner decisions as audit evidence.
- Makers: When a review lands, check who reaches your app through security groups, then keep, hand over or retire it without waiting for an IT clean-up project.
- Security/Compliance: Group-based access grows unnoticed as groups expand, so recurring owner reviews with a decision record are your evidence trail for app access.
