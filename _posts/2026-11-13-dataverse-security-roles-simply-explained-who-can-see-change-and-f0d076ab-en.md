## TL;DR

Dataverse protects business data with security roles, not passwords or firewalls. A security role works like a job description for data, deciding what each person can see, change and print. Permissions reach as far as five access levels define, business units mirror departments, and teams grant group access. Dataverse sits inside Microsoft 365, Azure and the Power Platform.

## Governance takeaway

- Admins/CoE: Design security roles deliberately, because one role decides what every person can see, change and print. Mirror departments with business units and grant access to groups through teams instead of stacking individual grants.
- Security/Compliance: Treat the security role, not a password or firewall, as the control protecting business data, and check how far each access level reaches before granting it.
- Makers: Understand roles, business units and teams before building apps on Dataverse, since permissions decide what users of your solution can do with its data.
- Leadership/Business: What looks like a small admin setting actually governs company-wide data access, and this is exactly where beginners get burned.
