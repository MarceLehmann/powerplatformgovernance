## TL;DR

The Agent 365 admin center brings AI agent oversight into an agent registry, with one list for every agent. Its dashboard shows usage, risk, and an agent map, while admins can review what they can do to agents. The episode also covers common mistakes, limits, and links to Microsoft 365 and Azure.

{% include curated-attribution.html %}

## Governance takeaway

- Admins/CoE: Use the agent registry as the central list, review ownership, and read usage, risk, and map views before managing agents.
- Security/Compliance: Treat unmanaged agents as a visibility risk, because ownership may be known only in a page most people rarely open.
