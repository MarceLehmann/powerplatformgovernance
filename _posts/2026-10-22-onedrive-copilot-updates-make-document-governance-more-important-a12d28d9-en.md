## TL;DR

OneDrive Copilot can analyse multiple spreadsheets, identify trends, compare information and create charts. SharePoint Admin Agent can assess governance and excessive sharing, while inactive SharePoint files can be archived through policies with what-if analysis before activation. Hero Links is adding simpler sharing and planned access expiration controls.

## Governance takeaway

- Admins/CoE: Use SharePoint Admin Agent to identify sites with excessive sharing and assess governance risks.
- Security/Compliance: Review permissions, inherited access and broad Anyone links before expanding Copilot use.
- Leadership/Business: Treat document structure and access quality as prerequisites for reliable, controlled AI results.
