## TL;DR

Power Platform RFP teams should decide go or no-go early, map every requirement in a compliance matrix, classify responses as Compliant, Partial, Custom or Not supported, and clarify licensing, integrations, data residency and security needs before pricing. The response should also show environments, solutions, pipelines, DLP and governance controls.

## Governance takeaway

- Makers: Mark each proposed capability honestly as configuration, custom or unsupported so delivery expectations remain clear.
- Admins/CoE: Show Dev, Test, UAT and Production environments, solutions, pipelines, Managed Environments and CoE practices in the proposed ALM model.
- Security/Compliance: Confirm environment region, Entra ID, roles, field security, DLP and auditing against the client requirements.
- Leadership/Business: Separate licensing from services and state assumptions because evaluators challenge unclear costs and scope.
