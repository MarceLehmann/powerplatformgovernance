## TL;DR

Power BI Premium provided shared capacity for many people opening the same report, with P1 through P5 sizes. The episode explains how Power BI now sits inside Fabric, why Premium is being retired, why capacity and licences are separate purchases, and how the F64 line affects viewer costs.

## Governance takeaway

- Admins/CoE: Separate the capacity decision from licence planning, because the episode identifies them as two distinct purchases.
- Leadership/Business: Review the move from Power BI Premium to Fabric, including the F64 line, because Microsoft is retiring the Premium name and model.
- Admins/CoE: Compare the current shared-report requirement with the available Fabric choice before migrating, because the episode warns against choosing incorrectly.
