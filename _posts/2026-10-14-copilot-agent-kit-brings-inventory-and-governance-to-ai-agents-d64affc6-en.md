## TL;DR

The Copilot Agent Kit builds on lessons from the Power Platform Center of Excellence Starter Kit and supports agent inventory, conversation analysis, debugging, compliance management, performance optimization, testing, prompt optimization, skills governance and knowledge-source review.

{% include curated-attribution.html %}

## Governance takeaway

- Admins/CoE: Use the kit to discover agents across the tenant, maintain an inventory and identify governance risks before the landscape grows harder to manage.
- Security/Compliance: Apply its compliance management and automated compliance capabilities, while using conversation analysis and debugging to investigate agent behavior.
- Makers: Use testing, evaluation, prompt optimization and performance tools to improve agent behavior and efficiency.
- Leadership/Business: Track usage-based AI costs and agent performance so expansion is considered alongside operational and governance needs.
