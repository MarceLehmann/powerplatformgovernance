Power Platform governance works best when it enables delivery instead of blocking it. The first step is to define a small operating model that makes ownership, risk, and decision-making visible.

## Start with clear responsibilities

Define who owns the platform, who manages environments, who approves connectors, and who supports makers. A simple responsibility matrix is often more valuable than a long policy document.

## Establish a minimum viable control set

Begin with environment strategy, data loss prevention policies, naming conventions, solution management, and a repeatable request process. These controls create a foundation that can grow with adoption.

## Measure adoption and risk together

A useful governance dashboard combines active makers, application usage, environment inventory, connector exposure, and unresolved policy exceptions. Governance becomes actionable when teams can see both value and risk.

## TL;DR

- Define platform ownership before scaling adoption.
- Start with a small set of enforceable controls.
- Measure business value and platform risk together.
