## TL;DR

Create interactive slides in PowerPoint Copilot can run governance quizzes, risk simulators and architecture builders. Agent Launch Control recalculates readiness and required controls from data sensitivity, action authority, autonomy and identity, while dangerous combinations can force a blocked state. The feature is preview-only and requires Frontier administration, compatibility testing and human validation.

## Governance takeaway

- Makers: Describe audience actions, states and consequences in prompts, then repair failed interaction models instead of merely restyling slides.
- Admins/CoE: Enable Frontier for all users or selected users and verify that licensed users can access the skill through Choose skills or its @mention.
- Security/Compliance: Treat scores, recommendations and review advisories as illustrative until the underlying method is validated, and review accessibility, fallback and compatibility risks.
- Leadership/Business: Use Agent Architect and related simulations to test architecture assumptions with decision-makers before implementation.
