## TL;DR

Michael Roth reframes the governance pitch per audience instead of leading with rules: to makers, it's "making sure your apps don't break when you're on vacation"; to managers, rules work "like traffic lights" that speed things up rather than slow them down; to IT, governance beats shadow IT by giving visibility without anyone becoming the villain.

{% include curated-attribution.html %}

## Governance takeaway

Swap your next governance rollout pitch from listing controls to naming what each audience actually loses without them — makers lose continuity, managers lose speed, IT loses visibility. The rules stay the same; only the opening line changes.
