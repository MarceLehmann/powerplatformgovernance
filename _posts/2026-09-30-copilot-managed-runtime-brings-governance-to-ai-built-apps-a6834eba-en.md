## TL;DR

Copilot Managed Runtime is a Microsoft-managed environment for Microsoft 365 line-of-business apps created through Cowork, Copilot Studio, or an SDK and CLI. Apps inherit tenant governance from creation, use Microsoft Entra authentication, appear in the Microsoft 365 admin center, and can be governed with Conditional Access, DLP, connector, sharing, and lifecycle controls.

{% include curated-attribution.html %}

## Governance takeaway

- Makers: Build through the managed runtime so AI-created apps start with organisational identity and policy controls.
- Admins/CoE: Review the Microsoft 365 admin center inventory, usage, health and lifecycle of apps regardless of their creation path.
- Security/Compliance: Configure connector, sharing, Conditional Access and DLP policies carefully; secure defaults do not correct weak configurations.
