## TL;DR

The portfolio offers a Security Quick Check covering access, DLP and owners through CoE or PowerShell evaluation, plus a least-privilege roles and permissions review and a custom connector review for API risks. Governance services include a kickstart, health check, admin training and ongoing advisory retainers.

## Governance takeaway

- Admins/CoE: Use the Health Check or Security Quick Check to evaluate the environment, access, DLP and owners before setting priorities.
- Security/Compliance: Review roles for least privilege and assess custom connector configurations to identify API risks.
- Makers: Use the Secure Maker Playbook or Secure by Design workshop to learn safer app and flow creation practices.
- Leadership/Business: Align governance and adoption through workshops that bring IT and business together.
