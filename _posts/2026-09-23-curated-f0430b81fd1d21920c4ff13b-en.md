## TL;DR

Self-service site creation removes IT bottlenecks but makes retirement the hard part: sites pile up with unclear owners and no activity for years. The fix isn't blocking creation — it's naming conventions and sensitivity levels set upfront, plus regular ownership reviews that catch sites whose owner has left the company.

{% include curated-attribution.html %}

## Governance takeaway

The same sprawl-versus-friction trade-off applies directly to Power Platform environments and apps — pair any self-service creation with lifecycle reviews and ownership attestation from day one, not after the backlog already exists.
