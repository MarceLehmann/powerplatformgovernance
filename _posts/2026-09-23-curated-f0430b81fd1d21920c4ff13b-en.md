## TL;DR

Self-service site creation removes IT bottlenecks but makes retirement the hard part: sites pile up with unclear owners and no activity for years. The fix isn't blocking creation — it's naming conventions and sensitivity levels set upfront, plus regular ownership reviews that catch sites whose owner has left the company.

{% include curated-attribution.html %}

## Governance takeaway

- Admins/CoE: pair any self-service creation policy with a recurring ownership-attestation review from day one — retiring an orphaned site years later costs far more than checking ownership quarterly.
- Makers: spinning up a new site, app, or environment should set naming conventions and sensitivity levels at creation time, not after governance comes looking for it.
- Leadership/Business: read sprawl as a lifecycle-cost problem rather than a request-friction one — blocking creation doesn't fix it, but skipping lifecycle review guarantees it.
