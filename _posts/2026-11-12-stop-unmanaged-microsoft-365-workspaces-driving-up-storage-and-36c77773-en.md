## TL;DR

Unowned, unused Teams, SharePoint sites and Microsoft 365 groups keep consuming storage and licences, because retiring one needs an owner decision. The routine: inventory every workspace with owner and last activity, assign owners where none exist, define inactivity, for example six months without change, ask owners to keep or archive, then report retired storage to finance. Savings arrive in steps after retention periods pass.

## Governance takeaway

- Admins/CoE: Inventory every Team, site and group with owner, last activity and storage, flag ownerless or single-owner workspaces, and repeat the review on a schedule so retirement becomes routine housekeeping, not a one-off cleanup.
- Leadership/Business: Have finance track retired storage and workspace counts each cycle; the first measurable saving arrives only after retention periods pass, so plan the effort over a quarter.
- Security/Compliance: Deleted content sits in recycle bins and can remain under retention policies for weeks, so keep a record of what was removed and why.
