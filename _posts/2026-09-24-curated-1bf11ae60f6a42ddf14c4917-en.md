## TL;DR

A custom Power Pages site can store English and French labels in a Dataverse custom table, load active records with Liquid FetchXML into a JavaScript object, and replace elements marked with content-key using the selected language. The pattern supports role- and page/user-property-specific labels, but header caching can return stale or inappropriate content; substitution may be needed.

{% include curated-attribution.html %}

## Governance takeaway

Makers should define the content-key and language-selection logic carefully, especially when labels vary by user role or page properties. Admins/CoE should govern the custom content table, its associations and deployment approach because business users manage the translation data. Security/Compliance should test role matching and header caching, since cached content can interfere with labels selected for signed-in users.
