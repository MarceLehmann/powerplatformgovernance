## TL;DR

Microsoft Copilot for legal work adds more than 20 specialized skills in Word and Copilot Cowork. It can review contracts against DOCX playbooks, suggest tracked changes, use approved precedents and extract obligations. Administrators must review Microsoft 365 permissions, Purview confidentiality labels and third party plugin data flows before deployment.

## Governance takeaway

- Makers: Use approved playbooks and precedents, then review every suggested Word change before accepting it.
- Admins/CoE: Check tenant availability, Microsoft 365 permissions, Purview labels and plugin deployment before enabling legal workflows.
- Security/Compliance: Assess third party data flows, permissions, contractual terms, confidentiality duties, GDPR and data transfers for each integration.
