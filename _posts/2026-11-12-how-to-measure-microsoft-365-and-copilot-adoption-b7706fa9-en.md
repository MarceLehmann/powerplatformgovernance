## TL;DR

Define active use consistently, such as activity in the last 30 days plus Copilot active days per month. Record a pre-change baseline for Teams, SharePoint, Microsoft 365 groups and Copilot, then compare licensed users, active users and licence cost by department. Review trends monthly or quarterly.

## Governance takeaway

- Admins/CoE: Agree a fixed active-use definition, capture the baseline before rollout, and report service and department trends on a monthly or quarterly cycle.
- Leadership/Business: Compare licensed users, active users, and licence cost to identify idle seats, target training, and support defensible budget decisions.
- Security/Compliance: Keep reporting at department and service level by default, explain what is measured and why, and avoid ranking individuals so adoption data supports help rather than surveillance.
