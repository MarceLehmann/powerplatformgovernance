## TL;DR

Use a fictional municipality or company for Copilot courses, based on the customer’s templates and tasks. Remove personal data, mark files as fictional, plant known errors, and keep an error-free control file so participants can test Copilot against an answer key without exposing real cases.

## Governance takeaway

- Admins/CoE: Use fictional files for shared Copilot exercises because participants may see uploaded content and files can remain in shared folders.
- Security/Compliance: Check personal and confidential information before any real files enter a course, because enterprise data protection does not control participant sharing.
- Leadership/Business: Tailor fictional cases to real templates and tasks so participants learn the method without shifting discussion into sensitive business cases.
