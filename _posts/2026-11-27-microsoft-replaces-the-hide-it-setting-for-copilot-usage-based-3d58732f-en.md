## TL;DR

Microsoft is retiring the AI experiences enabled by usage-based billing setting that kept Cowork and Code hidden from users without a spending policy. The replacement, Access to usage-based billing experiences, keeps the experiences discoverable and lets admins choose whether users can request inclusion in a spending policy or see more information. The change is covered in message center post MC1479277.

## Governance takeaway

- Admins/CoE: Check now whether you rely on the AI experiences enabled by usage-based billing setting to hide Cowork and Code; that hiding approach is retiring, so plan the move to the new access setting.
- Makers: Expect usage-based experiences to stay visible even without a spending policy and use the built-in request option to ask for inclusion when you need them.
- Leadership/Business: Settle budgets and approvers for spending policies before users start requesting access, since hidden AI options become a visible request flow.
