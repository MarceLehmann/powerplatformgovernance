## TL;DR

Microsoft Intune compliance policies evaluate device conditions and report compliance. The episode explains the two parts of Intune compliance, what a policy can inspect, what happens after failure, why the policy itself does not block anyone, and where compliance fits within Microsoft 365 and Azure.

## Governance takeaway

- Admins/CoE: Review what each Intune compliance policy can inspect and how failed devices are handled, because the episode highlights access disruption and common mistakes.
- Security/Compliance: Separate compliance evaluation from enforcement, because the policy itself does not block anyone even though a noncompliant laptop may lose work access.
- Leadership/Business: Treat device noncompliance as a potential work access risk and ensure the operational impact is understood before policies affect users.
