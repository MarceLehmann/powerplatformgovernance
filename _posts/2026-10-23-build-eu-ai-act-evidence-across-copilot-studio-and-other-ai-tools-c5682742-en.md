## TL;DR

Record every AI system and agent, including Microsoft 365 Copilot, Copilot Studio and SharePoint agents, with its purpose, reachable data, accountable owner, risk class and last review. Apply the same inventory, classification and confirmation cycle to external tools such as Claude Enterprise and ChatGPT Enterprise, then produce scheduled reports auditors can follow.

## Governance takeaway

- Makers: Record each agent’s purpose, knowledge sources and connectors so its data reach and accountability are clear.
- Admins/CoE: Inventory Copilot, Copilot Studio, SharePoint and external AI tools in one register, then schedule owner confirmations and reports.
- Security/Compliance: Classify systems by how they are used and retain evidence of oversight, monitoring and transparency duties where applicable.
- Leadership/Business: Require named ownership and retire agents without an owner or continuing purpose to reduce unaccountable AI risk.
