## TL;DR

SharePoint HTML pages may be created with Copilot or uploaded from another source, stored in the SharePoint Pages library, rendered, edited and shared. Before publishing, content owners must validate accessibility, branding, permissions, accuracy and compliance, while administrators should define creation rights, approval workflows, ownership tracking and site availability.

## Governance takeaway

- Makers: Validate accessibility, branding, permissions, accuracy and compliance before publishing HTML created with Copilot or uploaded from elsewhere.
- Admins/CoE: Define who can create or upload HTML, whether approvals are required, how ownership is tracked and where pages are allowed.
- Security/Compliance: Avoid assumptions about scripts, external resources, rendering behaviour and administrative controls because implementation details are not complete.
