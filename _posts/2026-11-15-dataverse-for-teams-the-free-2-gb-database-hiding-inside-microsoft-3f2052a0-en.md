## TL;DR

Dataverse for Teams is the real Dataverse, trimmed down and bundled into the Microsoft Teams licence most companies already pay for. Launched in September 2020, it holds two gigabytes of real data and lets people build apps inside Teams. Capacity and other limits arrive early, a common mistake catches almost everyone, and Microsoft changed things in 2026.

## Governance takeaway

- Makers: Check the two gigabyte capacity and the trimmed-down scope of Dataverse for Teams before committing a solution, because the first limits arrive early.
- Admins/CoE: Track where staff can build apps for free inside Teams licences and review what Microsoft changed in 2026 before standardising on it.
- Leadership/Business: App building on a real database already sits inside a licence most companies pay for, so Teams-based solutions can appear without new spend.
