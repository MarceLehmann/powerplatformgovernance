## TL;DR

Microsoft Agent 365 centralizes discovery, identity, monitoring, governance and protection for compatible AI agents. Microsoft Entra Agent ID gives agents distinct identities and separates technical owners from business sponsors, while Agent 365 Action Center can support responses such as reassigning ownerless agents or blocking agents when risks are detected.

## Governance takeaway

- Admins/CoE: Inventory agents across Microsoft and compatible third-party platforms, because unmanaged agents can create visibility and lifecycle risks.
- Security/Compliance: Apply distinct identities, least privilege and access reviews, because agents may access corporate information and execute actions.
- Leadership/Business: Assign business sponsors and measure costs, activity and outcomes, because technical operation alone does not establish business value.
