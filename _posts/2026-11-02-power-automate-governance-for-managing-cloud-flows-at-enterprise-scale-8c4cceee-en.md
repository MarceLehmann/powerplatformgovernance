## TL;DR

Power Automate governance should be designed early because cloud flows are easy to create and forget. Enterprise-scale management covers environments, DLP policies, ownership, connectors, monitoring and lifecycle, helping admins and CoE teams maintain visibility of automation risk, ownership and cost while supporting citizen automation.

## Governance takeaway

- Admins/CoE: Design Power Automate governance early across environments, DLP policies, ownership, connectors, monitoring and lifecycle because forgotten cloud flows can obscure risk and cost.
- Security/Compliance: Watch connectors and DLP policies as part of enterprise flow governance because the episode links them to maintaining an overview of risk.
- Leadership/Business: Support governance that preserves citizen automation productivity while keeping ownership and cost visible across the organisation.
