## TL;DR

Choose Agent Builder for one-person tasks, Copilot Studio for team solutions and Foundry for multi-agent or bespoke logic, custom models, private-data retrieval, production-grade requirements or engineering ownership. Establish guardrails, assign ownership to the business that knows the rules, and design human decision points into the process before building.

## Governance takeaway

- Makers: Choose Copilot Studio when team use, system actions and low-code delivery fit the need, but move to Foundry when custom logic or production-grade requirements exceed its scope.
- Admins/CoE: Define guardrails, lifecycle ownership and human decision points before agents are built, so teams can work at scale within clear parameters.
- Leadership/Business: Name the business owner who understands the process rules, assess the cost of errors, and start with desired outcomes rather than technology.
