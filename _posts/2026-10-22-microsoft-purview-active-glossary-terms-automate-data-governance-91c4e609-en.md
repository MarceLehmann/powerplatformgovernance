## TL;DR

Microsoft Purview active glossary terms attach security classifications, compliance rules, and access policies to data assets, columns, and critical data elements. Policies can be defined once at glossary level, while governance domains separate departmental meanings and critical data elements map different technical column names to one standardized business term.

## Governance takeaway

- Admins/CoE: Define policies at the glossary level and apply terms to assets so restrictions remain consistent without manually updating every table.
- Security/Compliance: Mark sensitive terms such as feedback results as restricted and verify that linked data products inherit those controls.
- Leadership/Business: Establish governance domains and standardized terms so departments can preserve business context while improving data discovery and AI trust.
