## TL;DR

Marcel Lehmann's tip highlights two connected uses of the CoE Starter Kit: reviewing access to apps and flows, and identifying resources left without an active owner. It points administrators to permission-management tools and an orphan-detection process that supports reassignment when someone leaves.

## Governance takeaway

Treat offboarding as a resource-ownership check, not just an account closure. Pair access reviews with a named successor for important apps and flows. Check current CoE component availability before applying this older tip.
