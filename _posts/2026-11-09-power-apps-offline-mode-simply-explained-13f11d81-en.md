## TL;DR

Power Apps offline mode requires a specific enabling choice, then lets an app keep working without internet. The episode explains why apps go dark when signal drops, what happens when connectivity returns, what a real offline app looks like, and which limitations and mistakes makers need to understand.

## Governance takeaway

- Makers: Identify the choice that makes Power Apps offline use possible, then design for work without a signal because connectivity can disappear in the field.
- Makers: Check reconnection behavior, offline limitations and common mistakes before release so users understand what the app can and cannot do when service returns.
